COMPANY PROFILE

Creative Couplings and Components is a manufacturer of self-sealing couplings under the popular brand name RAPILINKTM. Our product lines are used in flow lines for transferring variety of fluids at different temperatures and pressures. Pneumatic, Hydraulic and Special-purpose couplings are the different segments we cater too. We also manufacture check valves, non-return valves, grease fitting assembly and swivel joints. We services the premium market in industry verticals like Automobile, Space, Defence, Railways and Engineering Organizations to name a few.In the business of manufacturing self-sealing couplings, valve fittings and allied technology products. Creative Couplings & Components is an OEM supplier and pioneer in the manufacture of 'RAPILINK' self- sealing couplings. We have a huge clientele base spanning across different industry verticals like Atomic, Automotive, Defence, Engineering, Oil, Railway and Space..
